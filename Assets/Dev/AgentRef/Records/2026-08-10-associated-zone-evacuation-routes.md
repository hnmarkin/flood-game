Updated the evacuation prototype to route from shelter-associated zones instead of one global source zone. `EvacuationController` now chooses the first active shelter, reads its `associatedZoneGeoids`, finds an in-zone passable anchor near each zone center, builds simple A* routes to the shelter tile, and caches one `SimpleEvacuationRoute` per zone. `EvacuationRouteVisualizer` now pools and draws multiple simple LineRenderer routes with one color and clears old lines before redraws.
