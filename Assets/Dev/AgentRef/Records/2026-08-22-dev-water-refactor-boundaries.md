# Dev Water Refactor Boundaries

Implemented the refactor boundary cleanup, authoritative rendering, concrete barrier grid, deterministic bootstrapper, and role documentation. Troubleshooting found that RefactorScene's controller was initialized and stepping but dry because `TileMapData` loaded zero runtime-only `TileInstance`s; `TileInstance` is not serializable, so `Assets/Game` was left unchanged. The bootstrapper now regenerates the hard-coded map in play-mode `Awake`, before the controller starts. Persisted `WaterTileType.isWater = true`. Fresh play verified 24 wet tiles and water moving from the source into adjacent cells. Only the pre-existing URP settings exception remains.
