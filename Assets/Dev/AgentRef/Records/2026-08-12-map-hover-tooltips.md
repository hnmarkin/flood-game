Added MapHoverTooltipController to drive UXML-based evacuation route and communication tower hover tooltips. EvacuationController now sends hovered SimpleEvacuationRoute data to the shared tooltip, and CommunicationTowerController now sends hovered CommunicationTowerData plus status context. Tooltip UXML roots are cloned or reused, positioned near the cursor, hidden on hover/mode clear, and styled with simple USS.
